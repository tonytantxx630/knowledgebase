## Key points
- Continuation breakouts align with prior trend (triangles, flags, pennants) and are ideal for trend-following momentum strategies.
- Reversal breakouts move against prevailing trend (double tops/bottoms, head and shoulders) and require stronger confirmation before entry.
- Rising volume at breakout confirms conviction; low-volume breakouts have high fakeout risk and should be avoided or require additional confirmation.
- ATR (Average True Range) filters overextended moves and sets volatility-adjusted stops (typically 0.2-0.3x ATR buffer beyond swing points).
- RSI confirms momentum in breakout direction; divergence between price and RSI warns of potential fakeouts.
- Bollinger Band contraction before breakout often precedes strong expansion and signals compression ready to release.
- Never enter intrabar; always wait for full candle close beyond breakout level to avoid false signals.
- Aggressive entry: market order at candle close. Conservative entry: limit order at retest of broken level for better risk/reward.
- Stop-loss placement: below recent swing low (longs) or above swing high (shorts), plus ATR buffer to account for volatility.
- Take-profit targets: use measured move based on pattern height, or target next key structural support/resistance level.
- Risk 0.25% to 0.75% of account equity per breakout trade to maintain proper position sizing.
- Avoid trading within 2 minutes of high-impact news releases due to unpredictable volatility and spread widening.
