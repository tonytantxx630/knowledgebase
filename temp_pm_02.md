## Polymarket Whale Tracking and Order Flow Strategies

### Understanding Whale Activity on Polymarket

Polymarket's data is all public, it just isn't easy to visualize. Large traders (whales) can move markets significantly with their trades.

### How Whale Tracking Works

Whale tracking involves monitoring large bets (typically $10k+) in real-time to detect insider activity or high-conviction trades.

Key whale tracking tools:
- Polywhaler: Tracks large whale trades and suspicious activity in real-time
- PolyGains: Real-time insider trading detection, whale alerts, market analytics
- Chrome extensions for Polymarket whale tracking

### Identifying High-Signal Wallets

1. Look for wallets with consistent winning history
2. Monitor position sizing - larger positions = higher conviction
3. Track timing - trades before major news events
4. Check wallet history on Polygon blockchain

### Whale Copy Trading Strategy

Once you've identified high-signal wallets:
1. Track their wallet addresses on Polygon blockchain
2. Monitor new positions in real-time
3. Depending on confidence in the whale's knowledge, mimic direction
4. Consider position size as signal strength

### Risks of Whale Following

- Delayed signals (by time you see it, odds may have moved)
- Whale could be wrong
- Whale may have inside information you don't
- Front-running risk from other whale watchers

### Best Practices

- Verify whale track record before following
- Don't follow blindly - understand the event
- Diversify across multiple whales
- Set stop losses - whales aren't always right
