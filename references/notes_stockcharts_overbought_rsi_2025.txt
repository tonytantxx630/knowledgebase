Article theme: RSI “overbought” (>70) can indicate strength in uptrends; how to use RSI as a trend/momentum filter.

Key content (condensed):
- Common mistake: treating RSI>70 as automatic sell signal.
- In strong uptrends, RSI can stay >70 repeatedly (“clusters”), reflecting persistent momentum.
- Regime concept: in uptrends RSI often oscillates roughly 40–90; pullbacks toward 40–50 can act like support.
- Practical rules:
  - Use trend filter first (e.g., price above 200-day SMA).
  - Treat RSI cross above 70 as momentum confirmation (not exhaustion) when trend filter is bullish.
  - Look for breakouts/new highs alongside RSI strength.
- Additional tactic: consolidation where RSI cools from >70 but stays above ~50 can be bullish continuation.
- Risk: in sideways markets classic 70/30 mean-reversion interpretations work better; always respect price support/resistance.
