## Key points
- Technical analysis studies previous price movements and historical data to predict future price direction, primarily using price action on charts plus volume and technical indicators
- Fundamental analysis studies the CAUSE of market movements while technical analysis studies the EFFECT on asset prices
- Price action discounts everything: anything that can affect the price of an asset is already reflected in the current price
- Prices move in trends and trends tend to persist over time
- History repeats itself due to consistent human psychology and behavioral patterns in markets
- Support levels form when buyers emerge in a falling market at specific prices and push prices back up
- Resistance levels form when sellers enter at specific high prices and push the market back down
- Technical indicators are mathematical calculations based on price that provide information about trend direction (e.g., moving averages)
- Momentum indicators like RSI, Stochastics, and MACD measure the strength and speed of price movements
- Market volume and open interest can be used alongside price action to refine technical analysis decisions
