## Key points
- In M&A analysis, prioritize consolidating industries with few players and more demand than supply; look for multiple potential acquirers to create 'bonanza outcomes' through competitive dynamics.
- Markets systematically overstate the importance of signed definitive merger agreements; focus instead on actual behavior, resources, and incentives of the parties involved.
- A signed merger agreement with break-up fee provisions functions like an options contract—buyer purchases optionality while seller retains ability to respond to superior bids.
- Merger timelines: tender offers typically 1.5 months, normal mergers 3-4 months, deals with extended antitrust review approximately 1 year.
- Primary source research hierarchy: prioritize principals (CEOs, founders) over middle management, as principals lack organizational friction and fear of reprisal for speaking.
- Research preparation protocol: get 60-70% informed via primary documents (SEC filings, press releases) before contacting principals to maximize relevance and conversation value.
- Cold outreach value proposition must combine three elements: very nice (respect their time), very interesting (capital at risk, deep engagement), or very quick.
- Disarm information sources by explicitly stating 'You do not owe me an answer to this question'—removes defensive posture and allows voluntary cooperation.
- Information extraction through action-reaction: present 70% correct thesis to 100% informed source and read binary signals (tone, typing sounds, body language shifts) to confirm direction.
- Observable behavior signals trump verbal statements: whose airplane lands where (FAA tail numbers), whose office lights are on Saturday nights, who attends vs. skips press conferences.
- FOIA requests on waiver processes can provide advance notice on government contracts—target administrative prerequisites that occur before public announcements.
- Coordinate information gathering across team: assign non-overlapping subsets of questions to different people, then triangulate to extract maximum signal from binary responses.
- Non-tradable merger securities (contingent value rights) represent high-value prediction market opportunity—immensely valuable instruments with no liquid market and high uncertainty.
- Leverage political incentives for research: frame inquiries to state/congressional offices in terms of constituent job impact and electoral consequences to deputize officials as information sources.
- Bleeding hearts outnumber skeptics in capitalism—most principals believe their actions are righteous and will share openly if approached with genuine interest and low marginal cost ask.
