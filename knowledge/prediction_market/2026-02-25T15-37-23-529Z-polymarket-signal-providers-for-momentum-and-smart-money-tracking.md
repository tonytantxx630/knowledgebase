## Key points
- Polymarket Insider Bot tracks wallet activity and large trades for identifying smart money flow
- PolyFactual provides AI-driven market analysis with confidence scoring to validate trade ideas
- PolyBot enables execution-first Telegram trading for fast order placement
- PredictEdge offers AI edge detection with integrated Kelly criterion position sizing
- PolySignal matches breaking news to relevant markets for event-driven trading opportunities
- Use signal providers for entry and exit timing rather than solely for trade ideas
- Follow whale wallets for flow-based entries as large traders often have informational edge
- Implement automation through Telegram bots to reduce execution latency
- Combine multiple signal types (wallet tracking, news matching, AI analysis) for stronger confluence
- Kelly sizing from PredictEdge provides mathematically optimal position sizing based on edge size
- Signal providers supplement but don't replace fundamental market research
- Regularly audit signal performance to filter low-quality providers
- Consider signal latency when using automated execution systems
- Cross-reference whale activity with on-chain data for verification
