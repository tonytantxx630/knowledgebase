## Key points
- MACD strategy: Buy when 12/26 EMA MACD crosses above 9-day signal line, sell on cross below; ~60% profit rate with volume confirmation
- RSI divergences (price makes new high/low but RSI does not) signal weakening momentum; ~70% accuracy in trending markets
- RSI overbought threshold >70, oversold <30; look for mean reversion or trend continuation signals
- Bollinger Bands squeeze (volatility contraction) often precedes significant breakouts; combine with stochastic oscillator for ~55% profitability
- Golden Cross (50-day MA crosses above 200-day MA) signals bullish trend; Death Cross (50 below 200) signals bearish trend; ~65% trend identification accuracy
- Moving averages serve as dynamic support/resistance levels in addition to trend identification
- Stochastic oscillator (%K and %D) identifies short-term extremes: overbought >80, oversold <20; strongest in range-bound markets
- Fibonacci retracement levels (23.6%, 38.2%, 50%, 61.8%, 78.6%) anticipate correction endpoints within trends
- Confluence zones where multiple Fibonacci levels or indicators align at same price increase predictive value
- VWAP acts as dynamic intraday support/resistance; price above VWAP = bullish sentiment, below = bearish
- VWAP breakout with rising volume indicates institutional buying activity
- Combining indicators (e.g., Bollinger Bands + stochastic, MACD + volume) improves accuracy over single-indicator strategies
