## Key points
- RSI (Relative Strength Index) measures momentum with default 14 periods, scale 0-100, overbought above 70, oversold below 30.
- RSI Regime 1 (Strong Trend): RSI stays extreme (above 70 or below 30) for extended periods - trend continuation likely, don't fight it.
- RSI Regime 2 (Normal Range): RSI oscillates between 30-70 - mean reversion strategies apply, look for crosses of 50 for direction.
- RSI Regime 3 (Divergence): Price makes lower low while RSI makes higher low (bullish), or price higher high while RSI lower high (bearish).
- Hidden Bullish Divergence: Price makes higher low, RSI makes lower low - trend continuation signal, enter on RSI bounce from oversold.
- Hidden Bearish Divergence: Price makes lower high, RSI makes higher high - trend continuation signal, enter on RSI pullback from overbought.
- RSI Pullback Entry: In strong uptrend, wait for RSI to pull back to 40-50, enter when RSI turns back up.
- RSI Extreme Reversal: RSI below 30 at strong support with bullish divergence = buy signal when RSI closes above 30.
- RSI Crosses 50: RSI crossing above 50 indicates momentum shift upward; crossing below indicates downward momentum.
- Multi-Timeframe RSI: Weekly identifies trend, Daily finds entries, 4H times entry precisely.
