## Key points
- Information arbitrage - systematically processing public info faster/better than market consensus - is the only profitable arbitrage strategy in 2026 as bot-driven mechanical arbitrage dominates
- Bid-ask spreads compressed from 4.5% in 2023 to 1.2% in 2025, making traditional price-error arbitrage unfeasible for manual traders
- Specialize ruthlessly in ONE domain (sports, politics, crypto); build 90-day track record with $100-500 positions before scaling
- Define systematic, documentable, repeatable edges - 'good feeling' is not an edge
- For sports markets: monitor injury reports 4-6 hours before full adjustment, track weather conditions, analyze historical team performance in specific situations
- For political markets: aggregate polling from 8-10 sources daily, weight by sample size, historical accuracy, and recency
- Use Kelly Criterion for position sizing; enter trades when gap between your probability and market pricing exceeds 8-12 percentage points
- Cross-platform arbitrage (e.g., Polymarket vs Kalshi price differences) remains viable but requires fast execution
- Profit correlates more with systematic position sizing and trade volume than high win rate alone
- Top traders like Theo4 ($22M profit, 88.9% win rate) outperform 100% win rate traders by making more selective, properly-sized bets
