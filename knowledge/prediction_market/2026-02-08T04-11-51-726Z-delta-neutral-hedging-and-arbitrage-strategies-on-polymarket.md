## Key points
- Delta-neutral hedging involves buying a prediction market position (e.g., BTC Up) and simultaneously hedging with off-exchange futures to neutralize directional risk
- Profit in delta-neutral strategies comes from exploiting mispricings between prediction markets and spot/futures markets, not from directional moves
- Optimal entry point calculation is critical: hedge cost must not exceed potential prediction market payout for the strategy to remain profitable
- Pre-market predictions can be used for delta-neutral positions before official market opening, but face lower liquidity and higher slippage risk
- Cross-market arbitrage opportunities exist between Polymarket and mirroring platforms like Opinion
- Spread arbitrage mechanism: identify when sum of 'Buy YES' on one platform plus 'Buy NO' on another is less than $1.00, capturing risk-free profit
- Pre-market strategies require wider margin of safety due to increased spreads and execution uncertainty
- Cross-platform monitoring systems needed to identify transient arbitrage opportunities before they close
