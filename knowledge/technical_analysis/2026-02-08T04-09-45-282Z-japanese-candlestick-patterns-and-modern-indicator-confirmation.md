## Key points
- Bullish Engulfing pattern: red candle fully engulfed by larger green candle signals buyers taking control
- Morning Star reversal: three-candle pattern (long bearish, short doji/spinning top, bullish close >50% into first candle) indicates bottom formation
- Bearish Harami: small bearish candle within large bullish candle range signals trend exhaustion
- Use 20-period SMA or 50-period EMA to confirm trend direction before entering candlestick signal trades
- Confirm Morning Star reversals with positive MACD histogram shift for momentum validation
- ATR-based stop-loss: place stops at 1.5x ATR beyond pattern extremes for volatility-adjusted risk
- Bullish trend confirmation: ensure 50-period EMA is above 200-period EMA before taking long entries
- Stop-loss placement: set at engulfing candle low minus 0.5x ATR for buffer beyond pattern invalidation
- Target minimum 2:1 reward-to-risk ratio on candlestick pattern trades
- Risk 1-2% of account equity per trade maximum to preserve capital
- Maintain trading journal documenting time, candle type, entry price, and outcome for pattern performance review
- 1-hour timeframe effective for spotting engulfing patterns with sufficient signal quality
- Combine classic Japanese candlestick patterns with modern indicators (MACD, RSI, moving averages) for higher probability setups
