## Key points
- RSI above 70 indicates overbought (sell signal), below 30 oversold (buy signal); divergences between RSI and price signal trend reversals; use shorter 9-11 periods instead of 14 for crypto's higher volatility
- MACD bullish crossover (MACD crosses above signal line) generates buy signals; bearish crossover generates sell signals; divergence confirms trend exhaustion
- Bollinger Band squeeze (narrowing bands) signals low volatility and potential breakout; price touching upper band suggests overbought, lower band suggests oversold
- Volume Profile identifies Point of Control (POC) - price level with highest volume - as key support/resistance; reveals value areas for potential reversals
- Fibonacci retracement levels at 23.6%, 38.2%, 50%, 61.8%, 78.6% act as support/resistance zones; widely used in crypto making them self-fulfilling
- OBV confirms trends by showing whether volume flows into or out of asset; rising OBV with falling price indicates accumulation (bullish divergence)
- ATR measures volatility but not direction; essential for setting stop-loss distances in volatile crypto markets - use multiples of ATR for position sizing
- Golden Cross (50-day MA above 200-day MA) signals bullish trend; Death Cross signals bearish trend; moving averages act as dynamic support/resistance
- For trend trading: use moving averages for direction, confirm with MACD or Ichimoku, enter on pullbacks to Fibonacci levels or RSI oversold zones
- For breakout trading: watch Bollinger Band squeeze, confirm with Volume Profile POC break and OBV expansion, use ATR to set stop-loss at 1.5-2x ATR
- For range trading: identify range boundaries with Bollinger Bands, time entries with RSI/Stochastic at extremes, confirm with volume analysis
- Combine multiple indicators to confirm signals - single indicator false positives are common; require 2-3 confirmations before entry
- Stochastic Oscillator compares closing price to price range over period; values above 80 overbought, below 20 oversold; effective for timing reversal points
- Ichimoku Cloud provides all-in-one view of support/resistance, trend direction, and momentum; cloud thickness indicates strength of support/resistance
