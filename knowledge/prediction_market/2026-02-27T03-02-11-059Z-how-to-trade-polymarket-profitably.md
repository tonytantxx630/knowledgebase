## Key points
- Don't pay too much for 'obvious' outcomes - you can be right about the result and still lose money if entry price is too high
- Late entries get punished: when something feels obvious, the market has already priced it in
- Skip most markets - doing nothing is often the best trade, profitable traders take fewer setups
- Trade mispriced probabilities, not predictions - ask 'is this priced wrong?' not 'will it go up or down?'
- Think in probabilities: estimate actual odds (e.g., is UP really 70%?) rather than expressing confidence
- Short-term crypto markets (15m-4h) are fast, emotional, and volatility-driven - ideal for probability models, dangerous for gut trading
- Tools that help estimate real odds provide the actual edge, not automation bots
- If there's no clear edge, skip the trade - this single rule improves results more than any strategy
- Discipline beats activity in prediction markets
- Crowd emotion creates probability imbalances - look for moments where odds don't match underlying data
