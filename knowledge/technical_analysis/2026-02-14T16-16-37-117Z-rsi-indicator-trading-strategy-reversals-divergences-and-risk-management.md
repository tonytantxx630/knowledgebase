## Key points
- RSI measures momentum on 0-100 scale; standard overbought/oversold levels are 70/30, adjust to 80/20 for strong trending markets
- Long entry signal: RSI crosses above 30 with bullish candlestick confirmation and increased volume; short entry: RSI drops below 70 with bearish candlestick and volume confirmation
- Bullish divergence: price makes lower low while RSI makes higher low, confirm with bullish candlestick, volume increase, and resistance break; bearish divergence: price makes higher high while RSI makes lower high, confirm with bearish candlestick and support break
- RSI divergence strategies show 55-65% success rate when properly confirmed with price action
- Multi-timeframe framework: use daily chart (RSI above/below 50 for bias), 4-hour (reversals at 30/70), 1-hour (entry timing and momentum confirmation)
- RSI period adjustments by market: strong trends (14-21 period, 80/20 levels), range-bound (9-14 period, 70/30 levels), high volatility (5-9 period, 80/20 levels), low volatility (21-30 period, 65/35 levels)
- Stop-loss placement using ATR: long trades 1.5×ATR below entry, short trades 1.5×ATR above entry; position size = account risk / (ATR × multiplier)
- Two-tier profit targets: first target at 2×ATR, second target at 4×ATR; trail stops by 1×ATR increments and move to breakeven after first target hit
- Combine RSI with Bollinger Bands for exhaustion signals: RSI overbought + price touching upper band suggests potential reversal
- Trading style RSI periods: scalping/day trading (5-7 period), swing trading (14-21 period), position trading (21-30 period)
- Always wait for RSI to cross back through 30/70 levels before entry; acting on extreme readings alone leads to false signals
- Backtest RSI parameters across multiple market cycles with proper risk management, realistic trading costs, and out-of-sample validation to avoid overfitting
- Adjusting RSI settings based on market conditions can improve trade accuracy by 15-20% compared to fixed parameters
- Confirm RSI signals with additional indicators: moving averages for trend, MACD for momentum validation, volume for strength confirmation
