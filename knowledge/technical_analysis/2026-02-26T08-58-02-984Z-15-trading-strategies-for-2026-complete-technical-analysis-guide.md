## Key points
- Trend Trading: Enter on pullbacks in uptrends at support levels, exit when trend indicators reverse; captures large directional moves with defined risk
- Range Trading: Buy at support, sell at resistance in sideways markets; provides clear levels and limited risk in stable conditions
- Breakout Trading: Enter on confirmed breakout with volume confirmation, exit on retest failure or after target; catches early momentum at trend beginnings
- Momentum Trading: Enter when RSI/MACD confirm strong trend, exit when momentum diverges; captures entire strong trends
- Mean Reversion: Enter when price deviates significantly from moving average, exit when reverts to average; high win rate in ranging markets
- Fibonacci Retracement: Enter at key levels 38.2%, 50%, 61.8% during pullbacks in trends; mathematically defined reversal points
- Swing Trading: Enter after pullback confirms, hold for days to weeks, exit at momentum peak; balances time commitment with profit potential
- Scalping: Hold positions seconds to minutes, accumulate small gains; edge is volume over accuracy, compounding small wins
- Day Trading: Multiple entries/exits within single day, close all positions before market close; eliminates overnight exposure
- Position Trading: Enter on major trend confirmation, hold for months; low time commitment, captures major trends
- Price Action: Enter based on pure candlestick patterns and structure without indicators; universal applicability, no lag
- Gap Trading: Enter at market open after overnight gaps, exit when gap fills or reverses; captures overnight information
- Algorithmic Trading: Automated entry/exit based on pre-defined rules; speed, precision, elimination of emotional bias
- Match strategy to market conditions: trending markets suit trend/momentum/breakout, sideways suit range/mean reversion
- Always use risk management and backtest strategies before live trading; focus on one or two strategies initially
