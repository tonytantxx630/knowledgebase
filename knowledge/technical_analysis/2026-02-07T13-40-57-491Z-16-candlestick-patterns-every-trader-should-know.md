## Key points
- Candlestick anatomy: body (open-to-close), shadow (high/low), color (green/white for up, red/black for down).
- Hammer (bullish reversal): short body with long lower shadow (≥2x body), found at downtrend bottom; green hammers stronger than red.
- Inverted Hammer: long upper shadow (2x body), shows buying pressure followed by selling that failed to push price down; less reliable than hammer.
- Bullish Engulfing: small red body fully engulfed by larger green candle; signals buyers winning decisively.
- Piercing Line: long red candle followed by gap-down open, then green candle closes above mid-price of previous day; strong buying pressure.
- Morning Star (bullish reversal): three candles with short-bodied middle candle gapping between long red and long green; selling pressure subsiding.
- Three White Soldiers: three consecutive long green candles with small shadows, progressively higher opens/closes; very strong bullish signal.
- Hanging Man (bearish): hammer-shaped candle at uptrend end; lower shadow ≥2x body indicates bulls losing control.
- Shooting Star (bearish): small lower body with long upper shadow (2x body); market gaps up, rallies, then closes near open.
- Bearish Engulfing: small green body engulfed by long red candle; signals peak or slowdown; significance increases with depth of second candle.
- Evening Star (bearish reversal): three candles with short middle candle gapping between long green and long red; strongest when third candle erases first candle's gains.
- Three Black Crows: three consecutive long red candles with short/no shadows; sellers overtaking buyers for three sessions.
- Doji (indecision): open equals close, short/no body with varying shadows; neutral alone but found in reversal patterns; signals buyer/seller stalemate.
- Spinning Top (indecision): short body centered between equal-length shadows; signals consolidation/rest after trend; current pressure losing control.
- Falling/Rising Three Methods (continuation): small counter-trend candles contained within range of larger trend candles; trend remains intact despite minor pullback.
- Always confirm candlestick patterns with other technical indicators; patterns predict trends but require confluence for reliable signals.
