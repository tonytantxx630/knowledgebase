## Key points
- RSI identifies overbought (>70) and oversold (<30) conditions for potential reversals
- Bollinger Bands expand during high volatility and contract during low volatility, price often reverts to mean
- MACD crossover signals: bullish when MACD crosses above signal line, bearish when below
- VWAP provides fair value benchmark for intraday entries and exits
- Fibonacci retracement levels (23.6%, 38.2%, 50%, 61.8%) act as dynamic support/resistance zones
- ATR measures true range volatility to set stop-loss distances proportional to market movement
- Combine indicators from different categories (momentum + trend + volatility) for signal confirmation
- Always backtest any indicator-based strategy on historical data before deploying capital
- Stochastic oscillator complements RSI by comparing closing price to recent price range
- ADX measures trend strength regardless of direction, values above 25 indicate trending markets
- Ichimoku Cloud provides comprehensive view of support, resistance, trend direction and momentum
- No single indicator is sufficient; false signals require multiple confirmation sources
