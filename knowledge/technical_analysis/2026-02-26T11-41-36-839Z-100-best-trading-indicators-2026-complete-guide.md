## Key points
- Combine leading indicators (RSI, Stochastic) for early signals with lagging indicators (MA, MACD) for confirmation to reduce false positives
- Avoid stacking multiple indicators from the same category - use one from each of the four categories: trend, momentum, volatility, volume
- RSI 7-9 for scalping (fast, more signals), RSI 14 for swing trading (balanced), RSI 21-25 for position trading (smoother, fewer signals)
- MACD (8,17,9) for day trading responsiveness, (12,26,9) for swing trading balance, (24,52,9) for position trading smoothing
- Bollinger Bands mean reversion: buy when price touches lower band with RSI below 30 in ranging markets
- Bollinger Bands breakout: buy when price closes above upper band on strong volume after a squeeze in trending markets
- RSI + MACD + Bollinger Bands combo: Bollinger identifies setup (volatility compression), MACD identifies direction, RSI confirms timing not overextended
- Multi-timeframe RSI: use RSI 9 on 5-min for entries but confirm with RSI 14 on 1-hour to trade with larger trend
- No single indicator works in all market regimes - backtest and optimize settings based on historical data for specific market conditions
- Volume confirmation is essential - validate breakouts and trend signals with volume analysis rather than price alone
