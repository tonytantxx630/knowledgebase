## Key points
- Binary complement arbitrage: buy YES and NO shares when total cost < $1.00 for guaranteed profit at settlement.
- Multi-outcome bundle arbitrage: purchase all outcomes in a multi-choice market when sum < $1.00 to lock in riskless gain.
- Cross-platform arbitrage: exploit price differences for identical events across Polymarket, Kalshi, and other prediction markets.
- Catalyst momentum trading: enter positions immediately after breaking news before full repricing, exit into subsequent momentum.
- Term-structure spreads: trade price differences between similar contracts with different expirations to capture mispriced tail probabilities.
- Settlement-edge trading: analyze exact resolution criteria and decision logic to find bets mispriced relative to technical outcome rules.
- Correlation hedging: use related markets to offset directional risk and isolate relative value or basis reversion opportunities.
- Mention market 'NO' bias: fade overpriced YES in word-mention or long-shot markets where retail FOMO systematically overvalues unlikely outcomes.
- Favorite compounding: grind high-probability outcomes (>90%) where market overestimates risk relative to real-world data.
- Whale copy-trading: track and replicate trades of consistently profitable wallets identified via blockchain transparency and leaderboards.
- Position sizing discipline: limit individual trades to 1-5% of available market liquidity to minimize slippage and drawdown.
- Execution via limit orders: use limit orders and monitor real-time market depth to preserve edge and avoid excessive network costs or slippage.
