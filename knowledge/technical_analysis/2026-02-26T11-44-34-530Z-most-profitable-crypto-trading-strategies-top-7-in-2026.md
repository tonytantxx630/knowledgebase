## Key points
- Momentum Trading: Enter trades in direction of strong price movement using MACD crossovers; ride trend until momentum fades
- Mean Reversion: Buy assets when RSI drops below 30 (oversold); sell when RSI returns to 50 or enters overbought territory
- Breakout Trading: Enter on resistance break confirmed by volume spike; use tight stop-loss below breakout level; target previous swing high
- Range Trading: Buy at horizontal support, sell at resistance; use RSI overbought/oversold signals at boundaries; exit on strong breakout
- Trend Following: Identify trend using moving averages; only trade in trend direction; use pullbacks to enter; trail stop-loss as trade progresses
- Scalping: Execute very short-term trades (minutes) on 1-5 minute charts; focus on liquidity and tight spreads; requires high accuracy due to small profit per trade
- Swing Trading: Hold positions for days to weeks using daily charts to capture medium-term price movements
- MACD indicates trend direction and momentum; RSI measures overbought/oversold conditions; Bollinger Bands show volatility and potential breakouts
- Key candlestick patterns: Doji (indcision), Engulfing (reversals), Hammer/Shooting Star (pivots), Morning/Evening Star (trend changes)
- Risk Management: Never risk more than 1-2% per trade; always use stop-loss orders; maintain 2:1 or better risk-reward ratio
- Position sizing based on account capital; take profits at predetermined levels to avoid emotional decisions
