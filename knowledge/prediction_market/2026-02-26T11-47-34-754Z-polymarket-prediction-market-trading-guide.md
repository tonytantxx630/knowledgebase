## Key points
- Prices directly represent market-implied probability - trade when you disagree with consensus
- Binary outcomes resolve to $1 or $0 only; no middle ground - size positions accordingly
- Zero trading fees plus maker rebates enable capital-efficient liquidity provision
- Select markets with explicit, measurable resolution criteria to minimize oracle dispute risk
- Higher volume markets offer tighter spreads and better fills - prioritize liquid markets
- Pre-event position selling allows exit before resolution uncertainty
- UMA optimistic oracle handles resolution - understand this adds counterparty risk
- Stop-losses are problematic in binary markets; use position sizing as primary risk control
- Diversify across unrelated markets to mitigate single-event resolution ambiguity
- News-driven price moves create rapid volatility - enter before or during, not after
- Analyze historical accuracy of similar market types before committing capital
- Time decay affects positions as resolution approaches - factor into entry timing
- Low-liquidity markets can have extreme slippage - estimate slippage before large orders
