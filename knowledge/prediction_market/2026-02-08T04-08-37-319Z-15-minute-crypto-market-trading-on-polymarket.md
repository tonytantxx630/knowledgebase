## Key points
- Polymarket 15-minute crypto markets resolve on whether BTC/ETH/SOL/XRP price rises or falls, creating 96 daily opportunities per asset based on Binance spot prices.
- Markets are dominated by HFT bots (85%+ volume), requiring sophisticated strategies to compete profitably.
- Dynamic taker fees range from 0.20% to 1.56% depending on entry price, while maker orders pay zero fees and earn daily rebates from the taker pool.
- Price feed latency arbitrage exploits delays between fast WebSocket feeds and Polymarket price adjustments to capture mispricing.
- Momentum following strategy: bet on trend continuation if significant price move occurs in first 5 minutes of 15-minute window.
- Mean reversion strategy: fade extreme short-term spikes (e.g., 2% in 2 minutes) expecting pullback before settlement.
- Market making on both sides captures spread and rebates during low volatility, with top bots earning substantial daily rebate income.
- Position sizing should be 2-5% of bankroll per market to manage risk across high-frequency opportunities.
- Avoid trades when edge is eliminated: prices near 95c/5c after extreme moves indicate FOMO conditions with unfavorable risk/reward.
- Win rate above 52% is necessary for profitability after accounting for taker fees in typical trading scenarios.
- Optimal trading windows: US market open (9:30 AM ET) for volatility spillover, major macro events (Fed, CPI), and low liquidity hours (3-6 AM ET) for wider spreads.
- Resolution based on binary price comparison (close > open = YES wins) eliminates magnitude risk but requires precise timing on entry and monitoring.
