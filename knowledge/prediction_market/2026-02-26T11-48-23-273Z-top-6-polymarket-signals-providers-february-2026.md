## Key points
- Signal providers solve different parts of the trading workflow - wallet flow, alerts, arbitrage detection, news sentiment, and volume analysis
- Combine multiple tools for informational and execution edge rather than relying on a single provider
- Whale tracking involves following large, profitable wallets and identifying unusual activity patterns for alpha
- Arbitrage scanners detect cross-market inefficiencies by monitoring YES+NO sum deviations across exchanges
- News sentiment tools monitor market-relevant events and analyze sentiment correlation with price movement for event-driven trades
- Volume analysis platforms track volume by outcome and order book depth to identify institutional activity and market strength
- Manual traders should combine news alerts + volume analysis + portfolio tracker for optimal workflow
- Arbitrage traders need arbitrage scanner + volume analysis to detect risk-free opportunities
- Risk management requires never blindly following signals, verifying logic before execution, using proper position sizing, and setting stop-loss levels
- Signal fatigue is a real risk - monitor for burnout when following multiple alert sources
- Choice of tool depends on trading style (arbitrage vs directional vs market making), capital level, technical skill, and time commitment
- Portfolio trackers provide P&L tracking, win rate analysis, and position sizing recommendations for continuous improvement
