## Key points
- Market analytics: Track price/odds (65¢ = 65% probability), volume, liquidity depth, order book, and open interest to assess tradability and market sentiment.
- Wallet/trader analytics: Monitor whale P&L, win rate, trading volume, position history, and category performance to identify skilled traders worth following.
- Portfolio analytics: Track total P&L, ROI, win rate by category, time-based performance, and position sizing patterns to optimize your strategy.
- Win rate benchmarks: 50-54% breaks even, 55-59% is solid, 60-65% is excellent (top 10%), 65%+ is elite. Win rate alone doesn't determine profitability—average win/loss size matters equally.
- ROI targets vary by strategy: Active directional traders target 20-50% annually, market makers target 30-100%+. Compare performance to strategy-specific benchmarks, not arbitrary numbers.
- Maximum drawdown measures peak-to-trough decline. If historical max drawdown is 30%, size positions to withstand similar declines without blowing up.
- Volume insights: Markets with $50k+ volume offer better liquidity. Volume spikes often precede/accompany news events and indicate information flow.
- Third-party tools (e.g., PolyTrack) enable whale tracking, real-time alerts, detailed performance analytics, and watchlists that native Polymarket lacks.
- Whale following strategy: Screen for 60%+ win rates with $100k+ volume, analyze category strengths, set alerts, evaluate each trade independently—don't blindly copy.
- Consensus trading: Enter larger positions when (1) fundamental analysis supports outcome, (2) 2+ tracked whales positioned same direction, (3) price offers value vs. your probability.
- Category specialization: Review historical performance by market type, allocate more capital to categories with 55%+ win rate, reduce exposure to underperforming categories.
- Sample size requirements: Wait for 30-50+ trades before trusting personal metrics. For whale evaluation, require 50+ closed positions minimum to avoid small sample noise.
- Survivorship bias: Leaderboards show today's winners, not yesterday's losers. Look for consistent performance over months and check all-time P&L alongside recent performance.
- Analytics review frequency: Daily (whale alerts, positions), Weekly (P&L, win rate), Monthly (category performance, position sizing effectiveness, strategy assessment).
- Position sizing frameworks: Bankroll-based (1-5% per trade), conviction-based (size larger when signals align), performance-based (adjust after streaks), category-based (size larger in high win-rate categories).
