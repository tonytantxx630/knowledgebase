## Key points
- Large traders (whales) can significantly move prediction market prices - monitor large position changes for early signal extraction
- Information arbitrage is the primary edge in prediction markets - profit from superior information or analysis before the market fully prices it in
- Position sizing must account for manipulation risk - use smaller positions in thin markets susceptible to whale activity
- Research topics thoroughly before trading - platform-specific knowledge and event analysis provides durable advantage
- Platform competition creates promotional opportunities - watch for new user incentives and liquidity boosts during growth phases
- Liquidity varies dramatically across markets - enter positions early before whale activity creates unfavorable slippage
- Diversify across platforms to mitigate oracle and resolution disputes - no single platform should represent majority of exposure
- Monitor for suspicious trading patterns around high-profile events - potential insider trading signals can create temporary inefficiencies
- Regulatory uncertainty is an ongoing risk factor - factor potential platform shutdown or restrictions into long-term position sizing
