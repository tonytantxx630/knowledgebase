## Key points
- VWAP serves as an institutional execution benchmark - below VWAP indicates good fills, above indicates underperformance
- Institutions accumulate below VWAP creating support and distribute above VWAP creating resistance, confirmed by volume
- Volume Surge Retest setup: price breaks VWAP on 2x average volume, retests within 5-15 min with declining volume, enter on first green candle - 67% win rate
- VWAP Squeeze setup: price consolidates within 0.2% of VWAP for 30+ minutes with Bollinger Bands at narrowest point and volume at 10% session low - 71% win rate
- Failed VWAP Cross setup: price crosses VWAP on sub-20-period average volume, reverses within 3 candles, original side reclaimed on increasing volume catches false breakouts
- Pre-market gaps over 2% skew VWAP - use anchored VWAP from previous close instead
- Low float stocks under $1B market cap have insufficient institutional participation for reliable VWAP signals
- Avoid trading first 30 minutes and last 30 minutes of session due to auction distortions; optimal window is 10 AM to 3:30 PM
- Sector adjustments: widen bands to 1.5 std dev for tech (QQQ), tighten to 0.75 std dev for financials (XLF), anchor energy from Sunday night open
- Position sizing: risk 0.5% per trade, stop beyond next deviation band or 0.3%, profit target 1:1, trail with 20-period EMA, maximum 4 VWAP trades daily
- Multi-timeframe VWAP confluence: weekly monthly and daily anchored VWAPs aligning within 0.2% creates institutional decision points with 78% win rate
- VWAP Reclaim Trade: price spending over 2 hours below VWAP then reclaiming it on volume yields 73% probability of closing above VWAP
- Relative VWAP comparison against sector ETFs identifies institutional rotation - if SPY trades above VWAP but sector stock below, rotation is occurring
