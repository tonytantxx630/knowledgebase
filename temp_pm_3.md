# Polymarket Prediction Market Guide for Traders

## Summary

This guide explains how Polymarket works and how experienced crypto users can interact with its markets to express forecasts, manage positions, and assess risk. It covers the platform's technical overview, practical trading steps, and balanced discussion of benefits and limitations.

## Key Takeaways

- Polymarket is a permissionless platform for trading prediction markets
- Users buy and sell positions that resolve based on future events
- Prices reflect market consensus probability for an outcome
- Markets cover politics, science, macroeconomics, and measurable outcomes

## Platform Overview

### What is Polymarket?
- Global cryptocurrency-based prediction market
- Headquartered in Manhattan, New York City
- Launched in 2020
- Offers trading on sports, economic indicators, weather, awards, political events

### How Trading Works

**Step 1: Discover a Market**
- Choose an active market matching your research
- Consider time horizon and resolution date

**Step 2: Connect Wallet**
- Use compatible Ethereum wallet
- Ensure sufficient USDC balance

**Step 3: Execute Trade**
- Enter stake amount
- Position price shows implied probability
- Execute to receive tokens

### Understanding Prices

- Price = Market's implied probability of outcome
- $0.70 = 70% probability market assigns to outcome
- Price changes based on trading activity
- Final resolution determines payouts

## Risk Assessment

### Platform Risks
- Resolution risk - events may have ambiguous outcomes
- Oracle risk - UMA optimistic oracle handles resolution
- Regulatory uncertainty in some jurisdictions

### Trading Risks
- Binary outcomes = total loss or gain
- No middle ground - positions resolve to $1 or $0
- Liquidity varies by market
- Prices can move rapidly on news

### Position Management
- Monitor positions before resolution
- Consider selling positions before event
- Use stop-loss for risk management
- Diversify across multiple markets

## Practical Considerations

### Capital Efficiency
- USDC is the primary trading currency
- Zero trading fees (as of early 2026)
- Maker rebates available for liquidity provision

### Market Selection
- Focus on markets with clear resolution criteria
- Higher volume = better liquidity = tighter spreads
- Consider time until resolution
- Analyze historical accuracy of similar markets
