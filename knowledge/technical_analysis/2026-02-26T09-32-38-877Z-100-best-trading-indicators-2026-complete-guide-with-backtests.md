## Key points
- Combine leading indicators (RSI, Stochastic) for early signals with lagging indicators (MA, MACD) to confirm actual trend reversals
- RSI 7-9 settings: faster response for scalpers on 1-15 minute charts; RSI 14: balanced for day/swing trading; RSI 21-25: smoother for swing/position traders
- MACD (8,17,9): faster for day trading with more whipsaws; MACD (12,26,9): default balanced for swing trading; MACD (24,52,9): slower for position traders on weekly charts
- Bollinger Bands mean reversion: buy when price touches lower band with RSI below 30 in ranging markets
- Bollinger Bands breakout: buy when price closes above upper band on strong volume after a squeeze in trending markets
- RSI + MACD + Bollinger Bands combo: Bollinger identifies setup (volatility compression), MACD identifies direction (momentum), RSI confirms timing (not overextended)
- Multi-timeframe analysis: use RSI 9 on 5-minute chart for entries, confirm with RSI 14 on 1-hour chart to trade with larger momentum
- Avoid stacking multiple indicators from same category - they provide redundant information and increase false signals
- Use indicators from different categories (trend, momentum, volatility, volume) to get non-correlated signals
- No single indicator works in all market regimes - optimize settings through historical backtesting for specific assets and timeframes
