## Key points
- VWAP provides insight into institutional trading activity and where large orders are executing
- Use VWAP to determine intraday trend direction - price above VWAP indicates bullish bias, price below indicates bearish bias
- Buy above VWAP in uptrends, sell below VWAP in downtrends
- Place stop losses on the opposite side of VWAP from your entry
- VWAP calculated as Sum(Price x Volume) / Total Volume, reset at market open for intraday focus
- Use multiple VWAPs (daily, weekly) to confirm institutional flow across timeframes
- Combine VWAP with other indicators (RSI, MACD, volume) for confirmation
- Trade in direction of institutional flow - follow where large players are executing
- VWAP is primarily an intraday indicator and may not work reliably in low-volume periods
- Always use proper position sizing and never trade without stops
