## Key points
- Combine indicators from different categories: Bollinger Bands (volatility), MACD (trend), RSI (momentum) - avoid stacking same-category indicators that add noise
- RSI overbought >70 and oversold <30 are caution flags, not automatic signals; divergences at extreme levels (above 70/below 30) generate strongest reversal signals
- Bullish RSI divergence: price makes lower low while RSI makes higher low = selling momentum weakening, potential reversal up; bearish inverse applies
- MACD bullish crossover: MACD line crosses above signal line near zero = buy; histogram shrinking signals momentum decelerating before crossover occurs
- Bollinger Band squeeze (bands contract) = compressed volatility; 6-month low in band width = major move imminent, direction requires confirmation
- Mean reversion strategy: price touches lower Bollinger Band + RSI below 30 = buy expecting reversion to middle band (best in ranging markets)
- Breakout strategy: price closes above upper Bollinger Band on strong volume after squeeze = buy expecting continued momentum (best in trending markets)
- Multi-timeframe confirmation: use faster RSI (9) for entry timing on lower timeframe, slower RSI (14+) for direction on higher timeframe
- Tighter Bollinger Bands (1.5 std dev) increase sensitivity for mean reversion; wider bands (2.5 std dev) reduce whipsaws for extreme moves
- Centerline RSI crossover at 50 acts as trend filter: above 50 bullish, below 50 bearish - use to filter direction before taking signals
