## Key points
- Most prediction market traders lose due to improper position sizing, not poor directional calls; 91% of Polymarket traders fail because of sizing errors rather than inability to predict outcomes.
- Kelly Criterion formula for optimal bet size: f* = (P_true - P_market) / (1 - P_market), where P_true is the actual probability and P_market is the implied market probability.
- Professional traders focus on edge identification and optimal sizing rather than conviction or direction; they ask 'do I have an edge and how big is it?' instead of 'which way will it go?'
- Small edges require correspondingly small position sizes; a 4% edge on a 50/50 market warrants only ~2% bankroll allocation under Kelly, not the 5-10% retail traders commonly use.
- Kelly sizing maximizes long-term growth while minimizing risk of ruin and ensuring survival through inevitable losing streaks.
- Compounding small edges systematically beats large conviction-based bets; 50 trades with 4% edge at proper Kelly sizing yields +3.6%, 200 trades yields +14-15%.
- The critical challenge is accurately estimating P_true (true probability); professionals use historical distributions, volatility regimes, and systematic analysis rather than eyeballing charts or following sentiment.
- Oversizing on marginal edges is the primary failure mode; betting 5% per trade on near-coinflip odds leads to inevitable account depletion through normal variance.
- Kelly principles apply across platforms (Polymarket, Kalshi) and timeframes, though shorter intervals punish poor sizing more quickly.
- Proper bankroll management requires answering three questions: (1) What is the true probability vs market odds? (2) What is my optimal bet size given that edge? (3) Can I sustain this edge over many trades?
- Professional framework: identify edge, calculate optimal Kelly size, execute systematically, allow compounding to work, survive variance through proper sizing.
- Fractional Kelly (betting a fraction like 1/4 or 1/2 of full Kelly) is often used in practice to reduce volatility while maintaining positive long-term growth.
