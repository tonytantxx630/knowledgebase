## Key points
- Entry and exit indicators signal optimal times to open and close trading positions
- Key technical indicators include moving averages, Bollinger Bands, RSI, MACD, and key horizontal levels
- Ideal indicator setup combines a trend indicator for initial signal and an oscillator for confirmation
- Aggressive strategy enters at the first signal; conservative strategy waits for confirmation before entry
- Use ATR (Average True Range) for volatility-based exits to adapt to changing market conditions
- Support and resistance levels mark critical entry and exit zones where price may reverse or break through
- Rising volume confirms trend strength and validates entry signals
- Moving averages identify trend direction; crossovers generate entry signals
- RSI and MACD as oscillators detect overbought/oversold conditions and momentum shifts
- Bollinger Bands identify volatility expansion and mean reversion opportunities
