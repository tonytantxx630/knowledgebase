## VWAP Trading Strategy That Actually Works in 2026

### What VWAP Actually Tells You

VWAP (Volume Weighted Average Price) calculates the average price weighted by volume throughout the trading day. It's not a support/resistance level — it's an execution benchmark.

From an institutional perspective: A fund manager buying 500,000 shares needs to beat VWAP to justify their execution. Below VWAP = good fill. Above = underperforming.

This creates predictable behavior:
- Institutions accumulate below VWAP (creating support)
- They distribute above VWAP (creating resistance)
- But only when volume confirms their presence

### Three High-Probability VWAP Setups

Setup 1: Volume Surge Retest (67% win rate)
- Price breaks above/below VWAP on volume 2x the 20-period average
- Retests VWAP within 5-15 minutes
- Volume on retest is less than 50% of breakout volume
- Enter on first green candle after retest

Setup 2: VWAP Squeeze (71% win rate)
- Price consolidates in a 0.2% range around VWAP for at least 30 minutes
- Bollinger Bands contract to narrowest point of the day
- Volume drops to lowest 10% of session
- Enter breakout with stop at opposite band

Setup 3: Failed VWAP Cross
- Price crosses VWAP on low volume (below 20-period average)
- Immediately reverses within 3 candles
- Original side of VWAP reclaimed on increasing volume
- Identifies false breakouts and positions with real institutional flow

### When VWAP Lies: Market Conditions That Break The Rules

1. Pre-market gaps over 2%: VWAP becomes skewed. Use anchored VWAP from previous close.
2. Low float stocks under $1B market cap: Insufficient institutional participation makes VWAP unreliable.
3. First and last 30 minutes: Auctions distort VWAP. Wait until 10 AM, exit by 3:30 PM.

### Sector-Specific VWAP Adjustments

Technology stocks (QQQ): Widen bands to 1.5 standard deviations. Higher volatility requires wider bands.
Financial sector (XLF): Tighten bands to 0.75 standard deviations. Trade narrower ranges with more VWAP respect.
Energy: Anchor VWAP from Sunday night open, not daily. Captures overnight positioning.
Cryptocurrency: Use 4-hour anchored VWAP from local highs/lows. Standard VWAP almost useless.

### Building Your VWAP System

Pre-Market Preparation:
- Identify overnight range and gaps over 1%
- Mark previous day's VWAP close as S/R
- Set alerts at VWAP ±1 and ±2 standard deviations

Intraday Execution:
- Position sizing: Risk 0.5% per trade
- Stop placement: Beyond next deviation band or 0.3%
- Profit targets: 1:1 first target, trail with 20-period EMA
- Daily limit: Maximum 4 VWAP trades

### Advanced Techniques

Multi-Timeframe VWAP Confluence: Plot weekly and monthly anchored VWAPs alongside daily. When all three align within 0.2%, you've found an institutional decision point - 78% win rate.

VWAP Reclaim Trade: When price spends over 2 hours below VWAP then reclaims it on volume, probability of closing above VWAP jumps to 73%.

Relative VWAP Comparison: Compare stock's position relative to VWAP against sector ETF. If SPY trades above VWAP but AAPL below, institutions are rotating out.
