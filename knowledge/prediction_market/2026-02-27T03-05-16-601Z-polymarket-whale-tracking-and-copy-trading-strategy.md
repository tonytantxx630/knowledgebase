## Key points
- Monitor large trades ($10k+) in real-time to detect high-conviction whale activity
- Use tools like Polywhaler and PolyGains for real-time whale alerts and insider detection
- Identify high-signal wallets by analyzing consistent winning history over multiple markets
- Use position sizing as conviction signal - larger bets indicate higher confidence
- Check wallet history on Polygon blockchain to verify track record before following
- Copy trade by tracking whale wallet addresses and monitoring new positions in real-time
- Consider position size as proxy for signal strength when deciding entry size
- Trade immediately upon signal receipt to mitigate delayed signal risk from other whale watchers
- Understand the underlying event before copying - don't follow blindly
- Diversify across multiple whales to reduce single-source tail risk
- Set stop losses on whale-following positions - whales are not always correct
- Be aware of front-running risk from other whale watchers acting on same signals
- Whale trades before major news events carry higher signal weight but also higher inside info risk
