# Most Useful Indicators for Efficient Trading in 2026

## Chart Patterns - The Best Indicators for Anticipating Market Trends

While plenty of tools are gaining popularity, experts still rely on chart patterns as the most reliable indicators of future price movements. They help traders anticipate bullish or bearish trends and adjust trading strategy for both entry and exit points.

Chart patterns are even more efficient when paired with technical indicators like the Relative Strength Index (RSI), which measures speed and change of price movements.

## Key Chart Patterns for 2026

### Ascending Triangle
- Features horizontal resistance line and rising support line
- Indicates bullish pattern

### Symmetrical Triangle
- Forms when converging trend lines converge into consolidation period
- Signals continuation or reversal

### Bearish Flag
- Continuation of bearish trend
- Occurs during downtrend dynamic
