## Key points
- RSI overbought above 70, oversold below 30 - these are caution flags not automatic signals
- RSI bullish divergence: price makes lower low while RSI makes higher low = selling momentum weakening, potential reversal up
- RSI settings: 7-9 for scalpers, 14 default for all timeframes, 21-25 for smoother swing trading
- MACD bullish crossover: MACD line crosses above signal line near zero generates buy signal
- MACD histogram shrinking signals momentum decelerating before crossover occurs - earlier warning indicator
- Bollinger Band squeeze: bands contract together = volatility compressed, typically precedes high volatility breakout
- Mean reversion strategy: price touches lower Bollinger Band + RSI below 30 = buy expecting reversion to middle band in ranging markets
- Breakout strategy: price closes above upper Bollinger Band on strong volume after squeeze = buy expecting continued momentum in trending markets
- Combine indicators from different categories: Bollinger (volatility) + MACD (trend) + RSI (momentum) to avoid redundant signals
- Multi-timeframe RSI: use faster RSI (9) on lower timeframe for entry, slower RSI (14) on higher timeframe for direction filter
- Bollinger Band width at 6-month low indicates major move imminent
- Avoid stacking multiple indicators from same category - they add noise without new information
- ~95% of price action occurs within Bollinger Bands - use for context not just signals
