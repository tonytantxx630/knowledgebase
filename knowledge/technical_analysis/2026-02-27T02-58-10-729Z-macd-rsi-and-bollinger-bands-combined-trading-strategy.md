## Key points
- RSI overbought above 70 and oversold below 30 with 14-period standard setting
- Bollinger Bands provide dynamic support/resistance that widen during high volatility and narrow during consolidation
- MACD uses 12/26-period moving average crossovers to detect momentum shifts with documented 60%+ accuracy
- Enter long when price hits lower Bollinger Band, RSI below 30, and bullish MACD crossover aligns
- Enter short when price touches upper Bollinger Band, RSI above 70, and bearish MACD divergence appears
- Golden Cross (short MA above long MA) signals bullish trend reversal; Death Cross signals bearish reversal
- Volume-price divergence where price makes new highs but volume declines indicates weak momentum and potential breakdown
- Wait for confirmation across all three indicators to reduce false signals rather than relying on single indicators
- Altcoins use tighter parameters: RSI 25-30 oversold/70-75 overbought, BB period 15-18, faster MACD
- Mainstream coins use standard settings: RSI 30-70, BB period 20, MACD 12-26-9
- Stop-loss placement uses nearest support/resistance from Bollinger Bands with dynamic adjustment following price trends
- Position sizing of 1-2% risk per trade manages downside in volatile crypto markets
- RSI above 70 with shrinking volume (divergence) increases probability of imminent correction
- Short-term traders use 5/15-day MA crossovers for intra-day signals; long-term investors use divergences and mean-reversion patterns
