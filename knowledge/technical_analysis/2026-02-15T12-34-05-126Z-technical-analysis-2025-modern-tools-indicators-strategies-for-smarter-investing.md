## Key points
- Simple Moving Average (SMA) calculates average closing price over period (20/50/200-day); Exponential Moving Average (EMA) weights recent prices more heavily (9/21/50-day).
- Golden Cross (50-day EMA crosses above 200-day EMA) signals bullish trend; Death Cross (50-day EMA crosses below 200-day EMA) signals bearish trend.
- RSI measures momentum on 0-100 scale: >70 indicates overbought conditions, <30 indicates oversold conditions; modern systems combine RSI with sentiment analysis for confirmation.
- MACD tracks momentum by comparing 12 EMA and 26 EMA; MACD line crossing signal line (9 EMA) generates buy/sell signals.
- Bollinger Bands consist of moving average with upper/lower bands at 2 standard deviations; volatility clusters predict band expansions/contractions.
- Fibonacci retracement levels (23.6%, 38.2%, 50%, 61.8%, 78.6%) identify potential support and resistance zones.
- Confluence strategy: combine multiple indicators for stronger signals (example: RSI <30 + MACD bullish crossover + price >50 EMA = strong bullish setup).
- AI pattern recognition automates detection of chart patterns, candlestick formations, and market anomalies using LSTM neural networks.
- Risk management essentials: maintain 1:2+ risk-to-reward ratio, place stop-loss below support (longs) or above resistance (shorts), risk 1-2% of capital per trade.
- Modern platforms (TradingView AI, TrendSpider, Capitalise.ai) provide automated trendline detection, multi-timeframe scanning, and AI-driven alerts.
- Tools like QuantConnect and Tradytics automatically calculate optimal position sizing based on volatility metrics.
