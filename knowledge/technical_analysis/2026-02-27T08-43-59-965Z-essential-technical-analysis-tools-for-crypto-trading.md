## Key points
- Use EMA and SMA moving averages to identify trend direction - price above moving average indicates bullish trend
- RSI (Relative Strength Index) serves as momentum indicator for overbought (above 70) and oversold (below 30) conditions
- Bollinger Bands measure volatility - bands expanding indicate volatility expansion, contracting indicates low volatility periods
- Volume analysis confirms price movements - strong price moves should be accompanied by above-average volume
- Never rely on single indicators; use multiple indicators for confluence to increase signal reliability
- Confirm signals across multiple timeframes before entering trades to avoid false signals
- Indicator lag is inherent in moving averages - expect delayed signals in fast-moving markets
- False signals predominately occur in sideways/ranging markets - reduce position size or avoid trading in these conditions
- Backtest all strategies on historical data before live trading to validate effectiveness
- Combine trend indicators (moving averages), momentum indicators (RSI), and volatility indicators (Bollinger Bands) for comprehensive analysis
- Always implement proper risk management regardless of indicator signals
