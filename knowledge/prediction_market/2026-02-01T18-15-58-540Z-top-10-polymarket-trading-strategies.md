## Key points
- Binary complement arbitrage: Buy YES + NO shares when their combined ask price is below $1.00 for guaranteed profit at settlement
- Multi-outcome bundle arbitrage: Purchase one share of every outcome when total cost is under $1.00 to lock in risk-free gains
- Catalyst momentum trading: Capitalize on rapid repricing after breaking news by buying early repricing and selling into follow-through with mechanical exits
- Settlement-edge trading: Trade based on exact resolution criteria rather than headlines, exploiting gaps between narrative and contract rules
- Term-structure spreads: Arbitrage mispriced probability curves across same-theme contracts with different expiry dates
- Correlation hedging: Use linked markets to hedge directional risk and isolate relative value through basis reversion
- Cross-platform arbitrage: Exploit price discrepancies between Polymarket and competing platforms like Kalshi
- Favorite compounding: Grind high-probability (>90%) outcomes for steady returns while avoiding black swan tail risk
- Mention market 'No' bias: Fade overpriced YES shares in word-mention markets where retail excitement inflates probabilities
- Whale copy-trading: Monitor blockchain-transparent top traders and replicate high-conviction positions before broader market reaction
