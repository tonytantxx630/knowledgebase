## Key points
- Price action trading relies solely on price movement without indicators; all relevant market information is reflected in price itself
- Support is where buying pressure halts decline; resistance is where selling stops advance; breaks signal potential trend continuation
- Market structure: higher highs + higher lows = uptrend; lower highs + lower lows = downtrend; flat moves = consolidation
- Consolidation patterns (rectangles, triangles, pennants, flags) precede strong breakouts; identify early to prepare for impulsive moves
- Volume confirms move strength: high volume on breakout = reliable; low volume = weak move or false breakout
- Sequence of highs/lows strategy: enter pullbacks in trend direction, stop beyond recent swing points, exit at previous price levels; broken sequence signals reversal
- Trend following retracement entry: wait for pullback to key level, confirm with engulfing pattern, enter in main trend direction, stop below/above retracement point
- Breakout entry: enter long above resistance break or short below support break; require strong closing candle; stop just beyond breakout level
- Pin bar strategy: long wick rejection at support/resistance signals reversal; trade opposite to wick direction after close; stop beyond wick
- Inside bar strategy: smaller bar within prior bar's range during trend; enter breakout direction; stop opposite side of inside bar
- Head and shoulders reversal: three peaks with middle highest; enter short after neckline break; stop above right shoulder; target = head-to-neckline distance
- Entry methods: support/resistance levels, confirmed breakouts with momentum, candlestick confirmation patterns (pin bars, engulfing, doji)
- Exit methods: trailing stop-loss to lock profits, previous highs/lows as targets, reversal candlestick patterns as exit signals
- Avoid overtrading in choppy markets without clear direction; wait for strong trends or clean setups
- Always check higher timeframes (daily/weekly) to confirm dominant trend and key levels before trading lower timeframes
