## Key points
- Trend Persistence: Markets move in trends (uptrend, downtrend, sideways) that tend to persist; identify and trade in the direction of the prevailing trend.
- Price Discounting Principle: All available information (fundamentals, news, sentiment, psychology) is already reflected in current market price.
- Historical Precedent: Human psychology creates repeatable price patterns; past price behavior can be used to anticipate future moves.
- Support and Resistance Levels: Support represents buying demand at lower prices; resistance represents selling pressure at higher prices; use as entry/exit anchors.
- Trendline and Chart Pattern Analysis: Identify reversal or continuation signals using formations like head and shoulders, double tops/bottoms, triangles.
- Technical Indicator Confluence: Combine momentum indicators (RSI, Stochastics, MACD) with price action to confirm trade signals and avoid false breakouts.
- Volume Confirmation: High volume validates breakouts and trend continuation; low volume suggests weak moves that may reverse.
