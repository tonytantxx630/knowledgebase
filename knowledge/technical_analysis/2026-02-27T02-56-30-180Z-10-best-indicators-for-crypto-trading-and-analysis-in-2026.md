## Key points
- RSI above 70 indicates overbought (sell signal), below 30 indicates oversold (buy signal); use 9-11 periods instead of 14 for crypto's higher volatility
- MACD bullish crossover (MACD crosses above signal line) generates buy signals; bearish crossover generates sell signals
- Bollinger Band squeeze (narrowing bands) signals low volatility and potential breakout; band expansion indicates high volatility
- Volume Profile identifies Point of Control (POC) - price level with highest volume - acting as key support/resistance
- Fibonacci retracement levels (23.6%, 38.2%, 50%, 61.8%, 78.6%) provide effective support/resistance in crypto markets
- On-Balance Volume (OBV) confirms trends by showing whether volume is flowing into or out of an asset
- ATR (Average True Range) is essential for setting appropriate stop-loss sizes in volatile crypto markets
- Golden Cross (50-day MA above 200-day MA) signals bullish trend; Death Cross signals bearish trend
- For trend trading: combine Moving Averages for trend identification with MACD or Ichimoku for confirmation, enter on pullbacks using Fibonacci or RSI
- For range trading: use Bollinger Bands to identify range boundaries, RSI/Stochastic for entry timing, confirm with volume analysis
- For breakout trading: watch for Bollinger Band squeeze, confirm with Volume Profile and OBV, use ATR for stop-loss placement
- Ichimoku Cloud provides simultaneous information on support/resistance, trend direction, and momentum in one indicator
- Stochastic Oscillator compares closing price to price range over time, generating 0-100 values for reversal point identification
