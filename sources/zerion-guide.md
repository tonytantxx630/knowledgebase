## What is Polymarket?
Polymarket is a leading prediction market platform where you can trade on real-world events. Each market is structured around a simple yes-or-no question, and the prices represent the probability the crowd assigns to that outcome.

### How Polymarket works
Polymarket operates using "shares" that represent either side of a prediction. If a market asks, “Will BTC hit $150k in 2025?” you can buy YES or NO shares. Prices move based on demand, and your profit comes from buying low and selling high — or holding until the market resolves. When the outcome is known, winning shares settle at $1 while losing shares resolve to $0.

Polymarket runs onchain infrastructure on Polygon, uses a limit order book, and settles in USDC on Polygon. It does not currently charge fees and covers gas fees on Polygon.
