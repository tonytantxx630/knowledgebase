## Key points
- Cross-market arbitrage exploits price inefficiencies between different prediction market platforms
- Temporal arbitrage captures price movements before event resolution by anticipating market reactions
- 15-minute BTC momentum strategies can be adapted for crypto prediction markets to capture short-term predictive moves
- Copy trading with delay-adjusted entries allows following successful trader patterns while accounting for execution lag
- Kelly Criterion provides mathematical position sizing: f* = (bp - q) / b where b is odds, p is win probability, q = 1-p
- Always dry-run new strategies in paper trading mode before committing real capital
- Prediction markets face unique risks: geo restrictions (Polymarket unavailable in US), smart contract vulnerabilities, and liquidity gaps in smaller markets
- WebSocket order book monitoring enables real-time price feed analysis for rapid execution
- Automated execution removes emotional decision-making from fast-moving prediction markets
- Risk management should cap maximum position size at 1-2% of total capital per trade
