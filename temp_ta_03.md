## Ichimoku Cloud Trading Strategy: Complete Guide 2026

### What is the Ichimoku Cloud?

Ichimoku Cloud (Ichimoku Kinko Hyo) is a comprehensive technical indicator developed by Japanese journalist Goichi Hosoda in the late 1930s. The name translates to "one glance equilibrium chart" - providing complete market analysis at a single glance.

Unlike most indicators that focus on one aspect, Ichimoku shows trend direction, momentum, and support/resistance simultaneously.

### Five Components of Ichimoku Cloud

1. **Conversion Line (Tenkan-sen)**: Midpoint of 9-period high/low. Shows short-term momentum. Dynamic support/resistance.

2. **Base Line (Kijun-sen)**: Midpoint of 26-period high/low. Shows medium-term trend direction. Stronger S/R level.

3. **Leading Span A (Senkou Span A)**: Midpoint of Conversion and Base Line, projected 26 periods ahead. Forms one edge of cloud.

4. **Leading Span B (Senkou Span B)**: Midpoint of 52-period high/low, projected 26 periods ahead. Forms other edge of cloud.

5. **The Cloud (Kumo)**: Area between Leading Span A and B. Green when A > B (bullish), Red when A < B (bearish). Acts as dynamic S/R.

### Optimal Settings for Different Trading Styles

**Standard (9, 26, 52)**: Daily charts, swing trading - balanced view

**Fast (6, 13, 26)**: Day trading/scalping - more responsive, more signals

**Slow (12, 24, 120)**: Swing trading/long-term - fewer but stronger signals

### Trading Signals

**Bullish Signal** (all 3 conditions):
- Price above the cloud
- Cloud is green (Leading Span A > Leading Span B)
- Conversion Line above Base Line

**Bearish Signal** (all 3 conditions):
- Price below the cloud
- Cloud is red (Leading Span A < Leading Span B)
- Conversion Line below Base Line

**Signal Strength**:
- 1 condition: Weak - wait for confirmation
- 2 conditions: Moderate - proceed with caution
- 3 conditions: Strong - high probability setup

### Entry Strategy

**Long entries**:
- Wait for all three bullish conditions
- Enter when price pulls back to Conversion/Base Line and bounces
- Alternative: Wait for pullback that respects top of cloud as support

**Short entries**:
- Wait for all three bearish conditions
- Enter when price rallies to Conversion/Base Line and rejects
- Alternative: Wait for rally that respects bottom of cloud as resistance

### Stop Loss Placement

- Long positions: Below Base Line or below cloud
- Short positions: Above Base Line or above cloud

### Cloud Thickness Analysis

**Thick Cloud**: Strong S/R, difficult to penetrate, high trend confidence
**Thin Cloud**: Weak S/R, easily broken, potential for breakout
**Cloud Twist**: When Leading Spans cross - potential trend change warning

### Advanced Tips

- Strongest signals occur when all 3 conditions align AND price pulls back to Conversion or Base Line
- Combine with price action patterns rather than using alone
- Works across all markets: forex, stocks, crypto, futures
