## Key points
- VWAP shows average price weighted by volume, critical for intraday execution quality and key trading levels.
- Above VWAP indicates intraday bullish sentiment (buyers in control); below VWAP indicates bearish (sellers in control).
- VWAP serves as support in uptrends and resistance in downtrends - price tends to bounce from VWAP in trending markets.
- VWAP Bands (standard deviations): Inner band (1SD) is first profit target, Middle band (2SD) is key pivot, Outer band (3SD) is extreme overbought/oversold.
- VWAP Breakout: Price breaks VWAP with volume surge, retests and holds above VWAP = strong momentum entry.
- VWAP Mean Reversion: Price deviates significantly (>2SD), shows exhaustion signals, expect return to VWAP.
- Opening Range Breakout: First 15-30 minutes establish direction; breakout above/below range high/low with VWAP confirms momentum.
- Institutional logic: Buy orders executed below VWAP (better fill), Sell orders above VWAP - VWAP rejection may indicate distribution/accumulation.
- VWAP Fade: Extend >2SD from VWAP with reversal candles and decreasing volume = fade the move expecting reversion to VWAP.
- VWAP vs Moving Averages: VWAP reflects intraday volume-weighted average; EMA/SMA are time-based only - use both for confirmation.
