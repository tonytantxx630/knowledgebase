## Key points
- RSI measures momentum on 0-100 scale; use it as a regime filter and entry timing tool, not just overbought/oversold signals.
- In uptrends, RSI stays above 40-50; enter long on pullbacks to the 40-50 zone rather than fading overbought (>70) conditions.
- In downtrends, RSI stays below 50-60; use spikes into 50-60 zone as short entry signals for trend continuation.
- In ranging markets, trade mean reversion: sell overbought (>70-80), buy oversold (<20-30), target range midpoint.
- Regular divergence (price lower low + RSI higher low) signals potential reversal; price and indicator disagree on direction.
- Hidden divergence (price higher low + RSI lower low) confirms trend continuation; validates bullish trend strength.
- Momentum candle rule: candle body >2x previous candle size confirms momentum breakout or continuation.
- RSI 50 level acts as trend filter: above 50 = bullish bias, below 50 = bearish bias; stay in until 21 EMA crosses or RSI breaks regime.
- RSI identifies favorable conditions; wait for price action confirmation (structure break, momentum candle) before entering.
- Avoid fading strong trends: in uptrends, ignore RSI >70; in downtrends, ignore RSI <30; respect regime over absolute levels.
