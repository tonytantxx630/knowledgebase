## Crypto Technical Analysis: The Complete Guide to Mastering Market Trends and Indicators - 2026

### Core Principles

Technical analysis in cryptocurrency studies historical price movements to forecast future behavior. It operates on three key assumptions:
- The market discounts everything
- Prices move in trends
- History tends to repeat itself

### Price Action Trading

Price action trading is based on the market being driven by human emotion. Traders focus on:
- Higher highs and higher lows (bullish structure)
- Lower highs and lower lows (bearish structure)
- Rejection wicks showing strong defense at certain levels
- Consolidation zones before explosive moves

### Understanding Trends

Three primary types of trends:
- Uptrend: Higher highs and higher lows - buyers consistently willing to step in at higher prices
- Downtrend: Lower highs and lower lows - sellers dominate
- Sideways: Bouncing between support and resistance - consolidation

### Support and Resistance

Support is a price level where buying pressure stops a decline. Resistance is where selling pressure halts an advance. Key identification methods:
- Previous swing highs and lows
- Consolidation zones
- Psychological round numbers
- Moving averages

### Moving Averages

Simple Moving Average (SMA): Calculates average closing price over a set number of periods. The 50 and 200 SMA are popular for medium and long-term trends.

Exponential Moving Average (EMA): Gives more weight to recent price data, making it more responsive. Day traders prefer EMAs for short-term momentum tracking.

Golden Cross: When 50 SMA crosses above 200 SMA - bullish signal
Death Cross: When 50 SMA crosses below 200 SMA - bearish signal

### Key Indicators

RSI (Relative Strength Index): Momentum oscillator ranging 0-100. RSI above 70 = overbought, below 30 = oversold.

MACD (Moving Average Convergence Divergence): Trend-following momentum indicator. Crossovers signal buy/sell opportunities.

Bollinger Bands: Volatility bands around a moving average. Squeeze indicates low volatility (potential breakout), expansion shows increased volatility.

### Breakout Trading

Breakout trading requires:
- Strong volume confirmation
- Wait for candle close beyond the level
- Watch for retests of the breakout zone

Fakeouts occur when price briefly moves past a level, traps traders, and then reverses.
