## Key points
- China mapped US vulnerabilities by congressional district (agriculture exports, rare earths, pharmaceuticals) as countermeasures to tariff threats, demonstrating systematic preparation for trade conflicts.
- Expert assessment: Xi Jinping staying in power through 2025 is ~95-99% likely (barring health issues), suggesting markets pricing below 80% present value opportunities during rumor-driven volatility spikes.
- China has no negative incentive to stop supporting Russia's war in Ukraine—gaining battlefield intelligence, weapons testing data, and US distraction at minimal cost—implying continuation is baseline scenario until penalty structure changes.
- Taiwan resolution timeline: expert believes ~90%+ probability of 'resolution' within Xi's reign (next 10+ years), but rejects hard 2027 deadline narrative popular in media—markets may be mispricing near-term invasion risk.
- US Venezuela military intervention probability assessed at 5-10% by China expert (vs market prices often 20-40%), citing Trump's historical failures and blowback patterns in Venezuelan interventions.
- Asymmetric leverage shift: China's export controls on rare earths/magnets can shut down US factories in weeks, while US tariff threats now carry reciprocal domestic pain—second Trump term shows less US cost-free leverage than first term.
- Qualitative expert interviews reveal calibration opportunities: when domain specialists give extreme probabilities (5%, 95%+) that differ materially from market prices, investigate information asymmetries.
- China's dual-track Taiwan strategy: political accommodation preferred, kinetic options prepared—markets should price decade-long timeline with political resolution attempts frontloaded, not imminent military action.
- Rare earth magnets and high-bandwidth memory are critical bottlenecks in chip production—any relief on export controls would significantly accelerate China's semiconductor capabilities.
- Boeing aircraft purchases no longer provide Chinese leverage (no orders since 2019, Boeing backlog to 2031), demonstrating how strategic dependencies evolve and require regular reassessment.
- Agriculture leverage remains potent for China: soybean/sorghum purchase timing can create political pain for US administration via farmer constituency pressure in key GOP districts.
- Domain experts with granular on-the-ground knowledge (like China specialists) can identify when markets overprice media narratives—seek sources with decade+ immersion and primary language access.
- Prediction market strategy: wait for rumor-driven volatility spikes that push probabilities away from expert base rates, then take contrarian positions sized to information quality differential.
