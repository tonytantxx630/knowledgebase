## Key points
- Information arbitrage: profit by acting on news before it is fully reflected in market prices
- Cross-platform arbitrage: exploit price discrepancies between Polymarket and other prediction markets
- High-probability bond strategies: consistently trade near-certain outcomes for small, repeatable gains
- Liquidity provision: earn from bid-ask spreads and platform rewards by maintaining order book depth
- Domain specialization: develop analytical edge by focusing on specific categories (politics, sports, etc.)
- Speed trading: use bots and automation to capture millisecond-level price movements and react to market changes
- Only 0.51% of wallets achieved >$1,000 profit, indicating high competition and market efficiency
- Success on Polymarket requires targeting specific, exploitable inefficiencies rather than general betting strategies
- Analysis based on 95 million on-chain transactions, providing empirical foundation for strategy selection
