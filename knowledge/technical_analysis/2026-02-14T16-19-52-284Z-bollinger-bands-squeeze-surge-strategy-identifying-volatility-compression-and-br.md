## Key points
- Bollinger Bands consist of three components: middle band (typically 20-period SMA), upper band (middle + 2 standard deviations), and lower band (middle - 2 standard deviations); bands dynamically expand/contract with volatility changes.
- Critical mistake: treating bands as fixed support/resistance levels. During strong trends, price frequently rides along a band for extended periods without reversing, making band touches unreliable reversal signals in isolation.
- Free Bar reversal strategy: Enter counter-trend when an entire candlestick (body and wicks) forms completely outside the Bollinger Bands, signaling extreme volatility and potential exhaustion after extended moves.
- Free Bar entry mechanics: Confirm complete bar outside bands, prefer after sustained directional move, confirm with reversal candlestick pattern (engulfing, hammer, shooting star), place stop beyond free bar extreme, target 2:1 or better reward-to-risk.
- Bollinger Band Squeeze strategy: Identifies low-volatility consolidation periods when bands contract tightly, which typically precedes significant volatility expansion and directional breakouts.
- Squeeze breakout entry: Wait for decisive candle close beyond upper or lower band after prolonged squeeze, prefer simultaneous break of key support/resistance level, confirm with volume surge and strong breakout candle structure.
- Squeeze target calculation: Use measured move projection by measuring consolidation range height and projecting that distance from breakout point; alternatively target prior swing highs/lows.
- Timeframe-specific settings for day trading: Use 10-period SMA with 1.5 standard deviations for more responsive, tighter bands that capture intraday volatility and faster price movements.
- Timeframe-specific settings for swing/position trading: Use 50-period SMA with 2.5 standard deviations for wider bands that filter market noise and identify multi-day/week volatility cycles.
- Standard deviation measures volatility: The 2 standard deviation default captures approximately 95% of price action under normal distribution; adjust wider (2.5) for less sensitivity or tighter (1.5) for more signals.
- Volatility cycle concept: Markets alternate between low-volatility consolidation (bands contract) and high-volatility expansion (bands widen); squeeze strategy exploits this cyclical pattern by entering at expansion onset.
- Risk management for Bollinger Band trades: Always use stops beyond structure (free bar extreme, consolidation swing low/high), avoid entering without confirmation candle, and ensure reward-to-risk ratio justifies setup before entry.
