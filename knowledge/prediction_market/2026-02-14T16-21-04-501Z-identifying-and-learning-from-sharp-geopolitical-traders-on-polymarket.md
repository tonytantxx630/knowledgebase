## Key points
- Total profit rankings can be misleading—distorted by a few large winning trades rather than consistent skill.
- Win rate alone is insufficient for identifying sharp traders—easy to achieve high win rate by only betting on consensus forecasts.
- Blend win rate with total number of positions to identify consistently active and accurate traders rather than one-hit wonders.
- Top geopolitical traders on Polymarket often specialize in specific categories (Ukraine, Russia, Foreign Policy) and rank highly across multiple related markets.
- Sharp traders demonstrate conviction through position sizing—larger bets on outcomes they view as mispriced, not just high-probability consensus plays.
- Even top traders disagree on specific outcomes (e.g., territorial captures, leadership changes), indicating lack of consensus creates opportunity.
- Tracking active positions of sharp traders reveals their current convictions—useful for identifying potential mispricing or contrarian opportunities.
- Historical accuracy in specific market categories (e.g., 78% in Ukraine markets) is a stronger signal than overall account metrics.
- Using tools like Stand.trade and Polymarket Analytics to monitor sharp trader positions provides real-time edge identification.
- Copying trades blindly is insufficient—understanding why sharp traders take positions and their risk management approach is critical.
- Sharp traders often take contrarian positions on high-profile events (e.g., betting against Trump ending Ukraine war in 90 days) when market overreacts to narratives.
- Consistent activity across many positions (10-20+) in a category signals deep domain expertise rather than opportunistic betting.
