## Key points
- Bearish market bias confirmed when price trades below key level (end-of-2025) for 10+ weeks
- Use 50 and 200 EMA crossovers to confirm trend direction before entering positions
- RSI momentum indicator helps identify overbought/oversold conditions for entry timing
- Buy at strong support levels with tight stops; use rallies to add to short positions
- Always confirm trend on higher timeframes before taking trades - reduces false signals
- Volume profile validates support/resistance strength - higher volume at zones = more reliable
- Trend can reverse quickly on positive news - maintain flexible bias and adjust stops
- Proper position sizing is critical in trending markets to survive drawdowns
- Wait for trend reversal confirmation (broken resistance with volume) before switching bias
- Market neutral bias means neither long nor short bias dominates - use range-bound strategies in such periods
