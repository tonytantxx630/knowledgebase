## Key points
- Trendline breakout entry: Enter when price breaks above/below trendline, confirmed by reversal candlestick patterns (engulfing, rejection)
- Support/resistance entry: Buy at support levels, sell at resistance levels where price has previously bounced
- False breakout entry: Enter opposite direction when apparent breakout quickly reverses; requires expertise to distinguish from real breakouts
- Reversal pattern entry: Trade reversals using head and shoulders, double top, or double bottom patterns
- Stop loss exit: Pre-set automated order to exit when market moves against position to predetermined level, limiting losses
- Take profit exit: Pre-set automated order to lock in gains when price reaches favorable target level
- Trailing stop exit: Manual order that follows price in favorable direction, protecting profits from sudden reversals; requires experience
- Price action signal exit: Exit when reversal candlesticks (e.g., bearish engulfing in uptrend) indicate trend change
- Support/resistance exit: Close long positions near key support levels or short positions near key resistance levels
- Trading plan essentials: Must include clear entry/exit rules, risk management techniques, and continuous improvement process
- Consistency and discipline: Implement trading plan repeatedly without deviation, even during unexpected market movements
- Simplicity principle: Simpler strategies tend to be more profitable; avoid overly complicated indicator-based approaches
- Common mistakes: Trading without plan, making emotional decisions, using complex strategies, failing to set stop losses, ignoring price action signals
- Timing criticality: Accurate analysis can still result in losses if entry and exit timing is poor
