## Key points
- Entry/exit systems should combine primary signal with confirming signal within 2-4 candlesticks to reduce false signals and improve accuracy.
- Moving average crossover entry: fast MA crosses above slow MA while price holds above both, confirmed when RSI breaks above 50 level from oversold zone.
- Support/resistance bounce strategy: price bounces off level, retests it, then moves in rebound direction; aggressive entry on first breach, conservative entry waits for second level breach.
- Channel expansion signals trend start: after sideways movement, widening channel indicates new trend; confirm with increased volume and larger candlestick bodies.
- ATR-based exit: use average volatility to set targets (e.g., 40-point target on 60-point average range), close 50% at halfway point, protect remainder with trailing stop.
- Exit at support/resistance levels slightly before key zones where pending orders cluster to avoid getting caught in reversal waves.
- Reversal exit signals: divergence between price and indicator, oscillator entering overbought/oversold, trend indicator direction change, narrowing channels, reversal patterns.
- RSI signals: crossing 70 downward signals downtrend, crossing 30 upward signals uptrend, breaking 50 level confirms trend direction, divergence warns of reversal.
- MACD signals: line crossing above signal line is bullish (below is bearish), histogram above zero signals strengthening uptrend, histogram divergence indicates potential reversal.
- Bollinger Bands: contraction followed by expansion signals rising volatility and new trend, price breaking through widening bands shows accelerating momentum.
- Stochastic Oscillator: %K crossing above %D in oversold zone (0-20) is bullish, crossing below %D in overbought zone (80-100) is bearish, breaking 50 confirms trend.
- Day trading approach (M30-H1): catch trend start with MA crossover, hold until reversal, close before end of day to avoid swaps unless trend is strong, use MACD above zero for confirmation.
- Swing trading with Fibonacci: draw levels from first strong trend move high/low, trade retracements; aggressive captures moves between 0.236-0.5 levels, conservative trades only neighboring levels, price below 0.5 signals possible reversal.
