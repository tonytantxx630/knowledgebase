Article theme: key stock chart patterns; explains trendlines, continuation vs reversal patterns, and role of volume.

Key content (condensed):
- Trendlines:
  - Uptrendline connects higher lows; downtrendline connects lower highs; closing prices often used on daily charts.
  - More touchpoints (3+) increases validity.
- Continuation patterns (pause in prevailing trend): pennants, flags, wedges, triangles.
  - Triangle types: symmetrical (neutral breakout), ascending (bullish continuation), descending (bearish continuation).
  - General heuristic: longer pattern formation / larger internal move -> larger breakout move.
- Reversal patterns: head and shoulders, double tops/bottoms, triple tops/bottoms, gaps.
- Confirmation via break of boundary/neckline; volume often decreases during formation and increases on breakout.
- Measured-move logic: project expected move based on pattern height/structure after breakout.
