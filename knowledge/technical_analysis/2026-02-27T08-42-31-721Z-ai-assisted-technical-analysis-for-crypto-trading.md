## Key points
- Use AI to analyze sentiment from news and social media as input for trading decisions
- AI can identify subtle price patterns that human traders may overlook
- Employ AI for backtesting and optimizing trading strategies before deploying capital
- Treat AI predictions as probabilistic signals, not deterministic certainty
- Combine AI analysis with human judgment - use as decision support not replacement
- Process large volumes of market content efficiently using AI for sentiment mining
- Validate AI-identified patterns with manual chart analysis before trading
- Incorporate AI-driven risk assessment into position sizing and portfolio management
