## Key points
- RSI(14) achieved 87% win rate out-of-sample on Dow Jones from 1928-2024, making it the highest-performing indicator tested
- Bollinger Bands showed 81% win rate out-of-sample, indicating strong mean-reversion properties
- Donchian Channels achieved 78% win rate out-of-sample, effective for breakout trading
- Williams %R achieved 73% win rate out-of-sample, useful for identifying oversold conditions
- The best-performing indicators maintained high win rates in BOTH in-sample and out-of-sample periods, proving durability
- Use simple moving average crossover for trend confirmation before taking indicator signals
- Avoid overfitting by testing strategies on unseen out-of-sample data before deployment
- Longer timeframes (multi-decade backtests) provide more robust indicator validation
- High out-of-sample win rates indicate the indicator edge is not curve-fitted to historical noise
