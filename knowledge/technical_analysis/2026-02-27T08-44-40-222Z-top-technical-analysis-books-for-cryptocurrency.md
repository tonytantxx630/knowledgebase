## Key points
- Learn core indicators: RSI, MACD, and Bollinger Bands for momentum and volatility analysis
- Master chart pattern recognition to identify trend continuations and reversals
- Apply rigorous risk management including proper position sizing to preserve capital
- Start with foundational concepts before attempting advanced technical strategies
- Use demo accounts to practice and validate trading approaches without real capital at risk
- Maintain a trading journal to document decisions, outcomes, and lessons learned
- Develop a systematic, documented approach to avoid emotional decision-making
- Understand market cycles and valuation frameworks as context for technical setups
- Build systematic approach: define entry criteria, exit rules, and position management before trading
