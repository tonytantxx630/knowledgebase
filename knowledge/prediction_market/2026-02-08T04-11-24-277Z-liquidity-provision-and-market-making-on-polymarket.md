## Key points
- Polymarket liquidity providers supply both YES and NO outcome shares to facilitate trades and earn from bid-ask spread capture plus platform liquidity rewards
- Wide bid-ask spreads in illiquid markets create instant losses on entry/exit; tight spreads indicate healthy liquidity and lower transaction costs
- Two-sided liquidity provision earns nearly 3x higher rewards compared to single-sided orders under Polymarket's reward formula
- Orders placed closer to the current mid-price receive higher liquidity rewards than orders far from market price
- Systematic market making strategies analyze volatility across multiple timeframes (3h, 24h, 7d, 30d) to identify stable, high-reward opportunities
- Risk-adjusted ranking compares expected rewards per $100 invested against historical price volatility to optimize capital allocation
- Automated limit order placement with dynamic spread parameters based on market liquidity and volatility characteristics improves execution efficiency
- Market inefficiency exists where low-risk markets offer reward rates comparable to high-risk markets, creating arbitrage opportunities
- The Polymarket market making space remains underdeveloped with few automated systems, favoring early adopters of systematic approaches
- Open-source tools like poly-maker provide frameworks for data collection, market analysis, and automated order execution on Polymarket
