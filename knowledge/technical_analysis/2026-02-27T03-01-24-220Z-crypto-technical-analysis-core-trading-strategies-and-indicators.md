## Key points
- Higher highs + higher lows = bullish structure; lower highs + lower lows = bearish structure
- Rejection wicks indicate strong institutional defense at price levels
- Golden Cross (50 SMA crosses above 200 SMA) is bullish; Death Cross is bearish
- RSI above 70 signals overbought (potential sell), below 30 signals oversold (potential buy)
- MACD crossovers provide trend-following entry signals - buy when MACD crosses above signal line
- Bollinger Band squeeze indicates low volatility and potential breakout setup
- Breakout trading requires volume confirmation and candle close beyond resistance level
- Wait for retest of breakout zone before entering to avoid fakeouts
- Consolidation zones often precede explosive directional moves
- Support: price level where buying pressure stops decline; resistance: where selling stops advance
- EMA responds faster to recent price than SMA - preferred for short-term trading
- Identify S/R using swing highs/lows, consolidation zones, psychological numbers, and moving averages
