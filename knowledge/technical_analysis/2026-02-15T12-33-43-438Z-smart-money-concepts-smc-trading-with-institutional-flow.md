## Key points
- Smart Money Concepts (SMC) tracks institutional order flow by identifying order blocks (clusters of high-volume candles at key swing points), fair value gaps (imbalance zones that act as price magnets), and liquidity grabs (stop-hunts before reversals).
- Order Block identification: Look for consolidation zones with high volume near swing highs/lows or support/resistance where institutional orders accumulate before price continuation.
- Break of Structure (BOS) signals trend continuation when price breaks above a swing high (uptrend) or below a swing low (downtrend); Market Structure Shift (MSS) warns of potential reversal when price breaks counter-trend (above lower high in downtrend, below higher low in uptrend).
- Liquidity Grab strategy: Institutions trigger retail stop-losses at key levels (e.g., false breakouts) to accumulate positions before reversing price; trade the reversal after stop-hunt is complete.
- Fair Value Gap (FVG) trading: Enter positions when price returns to fill imbalance gaps created by rapid institutional moves, as these zones often provide high-probability reversal or continuation setups.
- Multi-timeframe approach: Use higher timeframes (Daily/H4/H1) to identify trend and institutional zones (order blocks, FVGs), then drill down to lower timeframes (M15/M5) for precise entry/exit timing.
- Breaker Block: A failed order block that signals trend change when price breaks through and continues opposite; trade the new direction after confirmation.
- Mitigation Block: Price retest of breakout level after sharp move; confirms breakout validity and provides re-entry opportunity in trend direction.
- Change of Character (ChoCH): Observable shift in market behavior following BOS, confirming potential trend reversal through altered price action patterns.
- Inducement: Institutional tactic creating deceptive price moves (false breakouts) near key levels to trap retail traders; wait for liquidity grab completion before entering counter-move.
- Supply/Demand imbalances from large institutional orders create zones of high buying/selling pressure visible on charts; these zones act as support/resistance for future price action.
- Algorithmic SMC implementation: Code pattern recognition for order blocks, FVGs, and liquidity grabs to scan multiple instruments; backtest rules incorporating swing high/low identification and order flow analysis.
- Top-down analysis workflow: (1) Identify trend on higher timeframe, (2) Mark key SMC zones (OBs, FVGs, liquidity pools), (3) Execute on lower timeframe within higher timeframe context.
