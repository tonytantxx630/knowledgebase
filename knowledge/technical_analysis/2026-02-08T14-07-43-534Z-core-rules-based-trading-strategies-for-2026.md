## Key points
- VWAP Liquidity Hunt: Enter on high-volume breakout followed by successful retest ('kiss') of VWAP line, using VWAP as fair value anchor for intraday trades
- EMA Cloud Strategy: Only trade long when 21 EMA > 50 EMA; enter on pullbacks into the 'cloud' zone between the two EMAs for continuation plays
- RVOL Flag Play: Identify vertical price move (flagpole) on high relative volume, wait for low-volume consolidation (flag), enter buy-stop above flag trendline break
- RSI Divergence Snap: Mean reversion setup triggered when price returns inside Bollinger Bands from outside extremes while RSI shows divergence from price action
- Level 2 Wall Scalping: Monitor order book for large bid/ask walls, front-run the break of these walls to capture panic covering from trapped traders
- News Second Wave: Avoid initial 5-minute bot-driven reaction to news; enter during subsequent human-driven consolidation if key support/resistance holds
- Pair Trading: Exploit temporary divergence between two highly correlated assets (e.g., KO vs PEP) by going long underperformer and short outperformer
- Covered Call Rent: Generate monthly income on existing long stock positions by selling OTM call options, monetizing time decay
- 1% Risk Rule: Professional standard limiting single-trade risk to 1% or less of total account capital to ensure survivability
- Stop-Loss Discipline: Non-negotiable exit points set before entry to protect capital and remove emotional decision-making
- Execution Speed Edge: Prioritize low-latency infrastructure and real-time news aggregation to compete with algorithmic trading systems
