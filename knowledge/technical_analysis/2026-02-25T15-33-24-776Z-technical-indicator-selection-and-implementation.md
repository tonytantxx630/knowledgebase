## Key points
- Technical indicators predict future price movements through mathematical analysis of historical market data
- Combine different indicator types (trend-following, momentum, volume, volatility) to increase effectiveness
- Select complementary indicators rather than redundant ones - avoid information overlap
- Limit the number of indicators on a chart to prevent analysis paralysis and false signals
- Core indicators to master: Moving Averages (trend), RSI (momentum), MACD (momentum/trend), Bollinger Bands (volatility)
- Avoid overloading charts with too many indicators - each should serve a distinct purpose
- Test all indicator strategies in a demo account before committing capital
- Quality over quantity: deeply understand a few indicators rather than superficially using many
- Indicators are probabilistic tools, not crystal balls - they identify potential setups, not certainties
